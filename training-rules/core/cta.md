# Core Training Rules: The Call to Action (CTA)

> Applies to every niche and every script. The final timing window of every script MUST be a CTA.
> A script without a clear CTA is an unfinished script.

## 1. What a CTA is

A Call to Action is the last spoken line(s) of a short-form video that tells the viewer **exactly one thing
to do next, how to do it, and why to do it now**. It turns attention into a result the speaker wants:
an order, a visit, a booking, a message, a sign-up, a follow, a comment or a save.

Short-form viewers do not guess. If the video does not tell them what to do, they scroll away and the
attention is lost. For a business owner the CTA is the most valuable sentence in the script.

## 2. Where it goes in the script

- It always sits in the **last timing window**: `[1:45 - 2:00] Closing Call to Action:` for 120s scripts,
  or the final bracket of shorter scripts (for example `[0:50 - 1:00] Closing Call to Action:`).
- It is 1 to 2 spoken sentences, 15 to 35 words. Never longer than the final window allows.
- It may be teased earlier ("stay to the end, I'll tell you how to get one at half price"), but it is
  only delivered once, at the end.

## 3. The anatomy of a strong CTA

Every CTA combines these parts. Use at least the first three.

1. **The action**: one clear verb. Send, tap, call, come, book, order, comment, save, follow, join, download.
2. **The channel / place**: exactly where. "WhatsApp the number on screen", "the link in my bio",
   "our shop opposite Total Molyko", "comment below", "DM me".
3. **The reason / payoff**: what they get. "your size reserved", "free delivery in Buea", "the full price list",
   "part two tomorrow".
4. **Urgency or scarcity** (only if true): "only 12 pairs left", "until Saturday", "this week only".
5. **Friction removal**: make it easy. "just send the word SIZE", "pay on delivery", "no account needed",
   "MoMo and Orange Money accepted".
6. **Specificity**: a real detail from the user's notes: product, price, location, landmark, opening hours.

Formula: **[Action] + [Channel] + [Payoff] + [Urgency/Friction] + [Specific detail]**

> Weak: "Contact us for more information."
> Strong: "WhatsApp the word SIZE to the number on screen and I'll reserve your pair before Friday's restock sells out, with free delivery anywhere in Buea."

## 4. Choosing the right CTA (decision guide)

Match the CTA to **who is speaking** and **what they want**. Read the user's notes for the goal.

| Speaker & goal | Primary CTA type | Typical channel |
|---|---|---|
| Sells physical products (shoes, clothes, perfume, food items) | Order / reserve | WhatsApp, DM, call, visit shop |
| Restaurant, bar, venue | Visit / reserve / order delivery | Location, WhatsApp, call |
| Service business (salon, barber, photographer, mechanic, tutor) | Book an appointment | WhatsApp, call, link in bio |
| Agency / consultant / freelancer | Book a call / request an audit | DM keyword, link in bio, email |
| Online course, ebook, digital product | Buy / join waitlist / get the free part | Link in bio, comment keyword |
| App, startup, SaaS | Download / sign up / join beta | Link in bio, app store |
| Creator / educator growing an audience | Follow / save / comment / part 2 | On-platform action |
| Event organiser | Buy ticket / RSVP | Link in bio, WhatsApp |
| Faceless or news channel | Follow for the next update / comment opinion | On-platform action |
| Lead generation for any business | Comment a keyword to receive something | Comment + DM |

Rules for choosing:
- **If the user names a channel** (WhatsApp, phone number, Instagram, website, shop address, email), use THAT channel.
- **If the user sells something and names no channel**, default to WhatsApp ("the number on screen" / "the WhatsApp link in my bio"); it is the dominant buying channel in Cameroon and most of Africa.
- **If the user's goal is to "attract more customers / users"**, the CTA must produce a measurable contact (a message, a visit, an order, a booking), not just a like.
- **If the user is a creator or educator**, prefer save / follow-for-part-2 / comment-a-question; these drive reach.
- One video = **one primary CTA**. A secondary micro-action is allowed only if it supports the first ("Save this, then WhatsApp me").

## 5. Rules (must follow)

1. Never end a script without a CTA.
2. Never use generic CTAs alone: "Contact us", "Check us out", "Like and subscribe", "Follow for more", "Don't forget to like".
   They may only appear as a secondary line after a specific one.
3. The CTA must be spoken naturally by the user, in first person, to the viewer ("send me", "come to our shop").
4. Use real details from the notes. Never invent a phone number, price, discount, address or stock level. If a detail is
   missing, use a neutral placeholder the creator can fill: "the number on screen", "the link in my bio", "our shop in [area]".
5. Urgency must be honest. Do not invent "only 3 left" unless the user said stock is limited; prefer soft urgency
   ("before the weekend rush", "while this batch lasts").
6. Match the payment and delivery reality of the market: Mobile Money (MTN MoMo), Orange Money, pay on delivery,
   delivery by moto / bike, pickup at the shop.
7. Keep it inside the last timing window and inside the word limit.
8. The CTA should connect back to the hook or the problem ("so your feet stop hurting by noon, send me your size today").

## 8. Urgency and friction lines (add to any CTA, only if true)

Urgency:
- "before Friday", "this week only", "while this batch lasts", "before the weekend rush",
  "the last restock sold out in two days", "only ___ places left", "before payday ends", "ahead of the festive season".
Friction removal:
- "pay on delivery", "MoMo and Orange Money accepted", "free delivery in town", "no deposit needed",
  "just send one word", "it takes 30 seconds", "try before you pay", "no account needed", "we come to you".

## 9. Building a non-generic CTA from the user's notes (procedure)

1. Identify the speaker: business owner, service provider, agency, creator, startup.
2. Identify the goal from the notes: "attract customers/users" means a contact or a sale, not a like.
3. Pick the channel: the one the user mentioned; otherwise WhatsApp for sellers, the platform action for creators.
4. Pick the payoff that answers the hook's problem (sore feet, so "the pair that lasts all day").
5. Add one honest friction remover and, if true, one urgency line.
6. Add one specific detail from the notes (product, area, landmark, hours, price).
7. Say it in first person, in 1 to 2 sentences, inside the final timing window.

Worked example. Notes: "I sell shoes in Buea, I want more customers."
`[1:45 - 2:00] Closing Call to Action: If your feet are tired of cheap pairs, WhatsApp me your size on the number on screen and I'll send you everything we have in stock today. Delivery anywhere in Buea, and you pay when the shoes are in your hands.`
