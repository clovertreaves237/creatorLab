# Core Training Rules: Hook Frameworks (0 - 3s)

> **Purpose**: Standard instruction set for Gemini short-form video generation (NFR-17).

## The 3-Second Retention Rule
Every short video must capture visual and auditory attention within the first 3 seconds (0-3s). Never open with generic greetings ("Hey guys", "Welcome back").

### Supported Hook Categories:
1. **The Negative Angle / Warning**:
   - *"Stop doing [X] if you want [Y]..."*
   - *"The biggest mistake creators make when [X]..."*
2. **The Counter-Intuitive Truth**:
   - *"Everyone thinks [X] works, but here is what actually happens..."*
   - *"Why [Common Wisdom] is dead in 2026..."*
3. **The High-Stakes Question**:
   - *"How did this Cameroonian founder scale from $0 to $10,000 without ads?"*
   - *"Is [Topic] really taking over?"*
4. **The Direct Demonstration / Result First**:
   - *"This single tweak doubled our watch time..."*
5. **The Direct Commercial & Venue Offer (Real Business & Restaurant Owners)**:
   - For real-world entrepreneurs, restaurant proprietors, food & drinks vendors, retail shops, and artisans advertising directly to local customers:
     - *"If you are in [Location] and wondering where to get genuinely delicious food and ice-cold drinks tonight, stop scrolling..."*
     - *"Tired of [Product] that [Common Pain Point] before you even finish your commute in [Location]?"*
     - *"The next time hunger hits you in [Location], remember this spot..."*
     - *"The secret to getting premium [Product] right here in [Location] without paying crazy prices..."*
   - **Critical Persona Rule**: If the prompt is from a business or venue owner advertising their own establishment/products, NEVER write a script lecturing them on branding or marketing. Write directly to their prospective customers, diners, and buyers!
