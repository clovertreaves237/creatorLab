# Core Training Rules: 120-Second Script Structure

> **Reference**: FR-31 (Capped at 120s spoken time) & FR-32 (Defined timing marks in brackets).

## Standard Timing Marks
- **[0:00 - 0:03] - The Hook**: Arrest scroll, state central thesis or tension.
- **[0:03 - 0:25] - The Context / Problem Setup**: Establish why this matters right now.
- **[0:25 - 1:15] - The Value Breakdown / Core Mechanism**: Deliver actionable, high-retention value using natural spoken bridges, sensory contrast, or causal storytelling. Strictly prohibit robotic counting or mechanical enumerations (e.g., "First, Second, Third", "Step 1, Step 2, Step 3", "Layer 1, Layer 2").
- **[1:15 - 1:45] - Concrete Example / Case Study**: Real-world application in African/Global creator context.
- **[1:45 - 2:00] - The Closing Call to Action**: Mandatory. One specific next step matched to the speaker's goal (order, visit, book, message, sign up, follow, save), with the channel and the payoff. Business owners get a contact/sale CTA (usually WhatsApp); creators get save / follow / comment CTAs. See `core/cta.md`.

## Formatting Output Rule (NFR-5)
The script output must be plain text with timing marks in brackets:
`[0:00 - 0:03] Hook: ...`
`[0:03 - 0:30] Context: ...`
`[0:30 - 1:30] Body: ...`
`[1:30 - 2:00] Close: ...`

## Dual Creator & Business Persona Engine (NFR-18)

Not every user on CreatorLab is an educator or consultant advising other business owners. CreatorLab serves both independent creators AND real-world business owners, entrepreneurs, restaurateurs, artisans, and product founders.

The AI generator must dynamically detect and adapt to the user's authentic speaker objective:

### Persona A: Direct Business, Product & Local Venue Owner (Commercial Advertising)
* **Trigger**: When the prompt mentions an actual business, restaurant, bar, food, drink, retail shop, physical product (perfume, clothes, shoes, jewelry), salon, local service, or venue (e.g., *"i have a restaurant in Buea Mile 18 and i sell drinks"*, *"i sell perfume oils"*, *"my bakery in Douala"*, *"my clothing brand"*), EVEN IF they say "promote my branding" or select the "Branding & Marketing" niche.
* **Speaker Perspective**: The business owner / founder / proprietor speaking directly in FIRST PERSON ("I", "my restaurant", "our kitchen", "we serve", "my shop", "our spot") with pride, hospitality, and authenticity.
* **Audience**: Prospective buyers, diners, patrons, local customers, and foot traffic in their city/neighborhood.
* **Core Goal**: Sell the food, drink, product, experience, atmosphere, or service to customers.
* **STRICT PROHIBITION**: NEVER generate a script where the speaker acts as an outside marketing/branding agency advising or lecturing business owners! The user IS the business owner! Do not lecture about logos, color palettes, or marketing theory. Talk about the food, the drinks, the products, the prices, and the customer experience!
* **Call to Action**: Direct customer buying / visit CTA: "Pull up to our spot at [Location] today, or tap the WhatsApp link in our bio to order / reserve your table right now!"

### Persona B: Creator, Thought Leader & Educator (Tactical Playbooks)
* **Trigger**: When the user is explicitly teaching a framework, analyzing trends, or sharing media playbooks without selling a physical consumer venue or product (e.g., *"3 mistakes in YouTube retention"*, *"tech ecosystem report"*).
* **Speaker Perspective**: Creator / Strategist sharing actionable insights with peers.
* **Audience**: Content creators, operators, and community peers.
* **Call to Action**: Community engagement CTA (comments, shares, saves).
