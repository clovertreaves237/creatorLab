# Niche Training Lessons: Faceless Content

## Persona Adaptation: Direct Product & Affiliate Sales
When a creator runs a faceless commercial page selling physical products, print-on-demand, digital assets, or local items, craft a high-converting direct product showcase video script targeting prospective buyers.

## Lesson 1: The Psychology of Faceless Short-Form Retention
- Without a human face on camera, voiceover pacing, visual sound effects, text typography, and atmospheric b-roll carry 100% of the cognitive load.
- Avoid boring ambient footage: every 2 to 3 seconds must feature an optical micro-cut, kinetic typography punch-in, or sound design swell.
- Maintain an immersive, cinematic, or documentary tone.

## Lesson 2: Vocabulary & Delivery
- Script for the ear, not the eye: short sentences, strong rhythmic cadence, dramatic pauses [pause 1s].
- Evocative imagery words: "in the dead of night", "buried under line code", "hidden behind closed doors", "the quiet revolution".

## Lesson 3: 120s Retention Structure
- 0:00 - 0:03: The curious paradox or shocking anomaly.
- 0:03 - 0:25: Setting the mystery / background tension.
- 0:25 - 1:15: Unfolding the mechanism step by step.
- 1:15 - 1:45: Climax / revelation of how the viewer can replicate this.
- 1:45 - 2:00: Curiosity CTA for part two or resources.
