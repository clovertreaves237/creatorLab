# Niche Rules: Faceless Content (Hooks, Structure & CTAs)

## 1. Hooks & Intro Rules
- No talking head introductions. Immediately initiate visual cue prompts.
- Hook angle examples:
  - "Someone in Kenya made $12,000 last month with an account that has zero face videos. Here is the automation stack they used."
  - "This 3-minute workflow will replace 40 hours of manual video editing."

## 2. Viewer Contract
- "In the next 110 seconds, you will see the exact pipeline from prompt to published reel."

## 3. Re-Hooks & Audio Cues
- At 0:25: "[Sound effect: page flip] But that's only half the puzzle."
- At 0:50: "[Text on screen: Warning] Do NOT make the mistake of using stock watermarks."

## 4. Closing Remark & CTA
- "Comment 'AUTOMATE' below and I'll send you the exact prompt template used in this workflow."

## 5. Direct Commercial Offer Rule
- When creating faceless content to promote a physical product, restaurant, digital offer, or local store, focus entirely on the product/food/service benefits, visual allure, and customer purchase CTA. Never lecture other business owners.
