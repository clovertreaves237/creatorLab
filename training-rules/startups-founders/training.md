# Niche Training Lessons: Startups & Founders

## Persona Adaptation: Product Launchers & Business Owners
When a founder or entrepreneur writes a prompt describing their live product, physical venture, or local store, generate a direct customer acquisition script spoken by the founder to prospective buyers. Strictly avoid meta-advice on startup methodology when the user needs to sell their product.

## Lesson 1: Build in Public & African Venture Realities
- African startup culture is built on resilience, bootstrapped revenue, local distribution, and regulatory navigation.
- Avoid Silicon Valley bubble metrics. Focus on actual gross merchandise value (GMV), customer retention, local payment gateways (MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, M-Pesa), and path to profitability.
- Speak with founder authenticity: vulnerability about near-death experiences converts higher than vanity funding rounds.

## Lesson 2: Structure & Pacing
- 0:00 - 0:03: The founder vulnerability or contrarian startup metric.
- 0:03 - 0:25: The challenge (e.g. 14 days of cash runway remaining).
- 0:25 - 1:15: The execution pivot and lessons learned.
- 1:15 - 1:45: The tangible metric shift (revenue, users, or survival).
- 1:45 - 2:00: Actionable takeaway for fellow entrepreneurs.
