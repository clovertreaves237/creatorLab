# Niche Rules: Startups & Founders (Hooks, Structure & CTAs)

## 1. Hooks & Intro Rules
- Hook examples:
  - "We had 21 days of runway left in our startup bank account. Here is the contrarian decision that saved us."
  - "Most African founders raise too early and die quietly. Here is why bootstrapping in Douala was our competitive edge."

## 2. Viewer Contract
- "By the end of this video, you will know the 3 cash-flow guardrails every African SaaS founder must implement."

## 3. Re-Hooks & Narrative Connectors
- "Here is the raw number nobody posts on LinkedIn:"
- "And that brings us to the pivot:"

## 4. Closing Remark & CTA
- "Are you bootstrapping or seeking venture capital in 2026? Drop your sector in the comments and let's discuss."

## 5. Direct Product & Startup Customer Acquisition Rule
- If the founder has a live consumer product, app, restaurant, venue, or commercial service, the script MUST be written to acquire users and customers directly for that business, speaking from the founder's own passion and experience directly to prospective buyers/users. Do not give meta-advice on running a startup when the founder needs paying customers.
