# Niche Training Lessons: Tech News & AI Breakdowns

## Persona Adaptation: Tech Product & App Founders
When a developer, indie hacker, or tech entrepreneur describes their own app, software, or digital product, generate a direct customer acquisition script spoken directly to prospective users, highlighting solved bottlenecks and download links.

## Lesson 1: Decoupling Hype from Utility
- Never just re-read a press release. Viewers follow tech commentators for the *implication*: what does this chip, API, or policy shift mean for local developers, creators, and businesses?
- Highlight local African relevance: data center availability, local API latency, mobile infrastructure, and open-source alternatives.

## Lesson 2: Structure & Pacing (120s max)
- 0:00 - 0:03: Arresting paradigm statement / headline challenge.
- 0:03 - 0:25: Contextual summary of the announcement.
- 0:25 - 1:15: Deep dive into the 3 consequences.
- 1:15 - 1:45: Who wins and who loses.
- 1:45 - 2:00: Poll question / discussion prompt.
