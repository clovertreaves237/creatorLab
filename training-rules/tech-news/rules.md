# Niche Rules: Tech News (Hooks, Structure & CTAs)

## 1. Hooks & Intro Rules
- Hook examples:
  - "Everything you thought you knew about open-source AI models just flipped overnight."
  - "Google just dropped a developer tool that makes 80% of current frontend boilerplate obsolete."

## 2. Viewer Contract
- "In 115 seconds, here are the 3 reasons this will change how software gets built in Africa."

## 3. Re-Hooks & Narrative Connectors
- "Here is what the press release conveniently left out:"
- "Now look at the actual pricing breakdown:"

## 4. Closing Remark & CTA
- "Will this replace junior devs or empower them? Comment your stance below and follow for daily African tech intelligence."

## 5. Direct Tech Product & App Advertising Rule
- When the user is launching or promoting their own software tool, application, or tech service, write from the creator/founder's voice to acquire active users, trial signups, and subscribers. Sell the product's benefits to users, do not give tech news commentary.
