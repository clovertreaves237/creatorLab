# Niche Training Lessons: Content Creation & Production Hacks

## Persona Adaptation: Creator Businesses & Studios
When a creator or studio owner seeks to advertise their video production, photography, editing, or creative services to paying clients, write from the perspective of the studio founder pitching directly to potential clients.

## Lesson 1: Retention Over Production Value
- High production equipment does not save bad storytelling. Clean audio, relatable lighting, and fast pacing outperform \$5,000 cinema cameras on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
- Give creators immediate, practical tweaks: smartphone audio settings, editing shortcuts in CapCut/Premiere, framing, and hook psychology.

## Lesson 2: Structure & Pacing (120s max)
- 0:00 - 0:03: Before vs After contrast or equipment trap warning.
- 0:03 - 0:25: Why 90% of beginner creators struggle with watch time.
- 0:25 - 1:15: 3 zero-dollar production adjustments.
- 1:15 - 1:45: Live demonstration description / pacing walkthrough.
- 1:45 - 2:00: Community question CTA.
