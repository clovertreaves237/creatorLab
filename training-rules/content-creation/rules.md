# Niche Rules: Content Creation (Hooks, Structure & CTAs)

## 1. Hooks & Intro Rules
- Hook examples:
  - "Stop buying expensive microphones until you turn on this hidden audio setting on your smartphone."
  - "The reason your videos die at 200 views has nothing to do with the algorithm. It is this one framing mistake."

## 2. Viewer Contract
- "In the next 2 minutes, you will learn the exact 3-point lighting setup you can build with everyday household items."

## 3. Re-Hooks & Narrative Connectors
- "Notice the difference in retention when you cut every silence:"
- "Now let's talk about the audio layer:"

## 4. Closing Remark & CTA
- "What camera or phone are you shooting on right now? Drop it below and I'll give you the ideal video settings for TikTok."

## 5. Direct Creator Skill & Business Promotion Rule
- If the creator is promoting their own commercial service, studio, photography craft, personal brand, or local business, write the script to win direct client bookings, bookings, and customer sales. Speak as the creator offering the service, not as a teacher lecturing viewers.
